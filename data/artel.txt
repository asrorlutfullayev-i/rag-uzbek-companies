== Overview ==
Artel Electronics is a company based in Uzbekistan that manufactures consumer electronics. Its headquarters are in Tashkent.

== History ==
Artel set up its first factory in Tashkent in 2011. That first factory produced gas stoves.
In 2012 the company released its first smartphone.

== Credit rating ==
In 2021 Fitch Ratings gave Artel a long-term issuer default rating of "B". In the following year (2022) Artel received the same "B" rating again.
